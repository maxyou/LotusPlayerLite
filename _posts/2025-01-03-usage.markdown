The interface is very intuitive, and most functions are easy to use at a glance. I will only introduce some of the key features.

### 1. Multiple Selection
The three dots in the upper right corner can be used to enable "multiple selection". After selecting multiple files, click the "command clover" to perform operations on multiple files.

### 2. Timer
The three dots in the upper right corner can be used to enable the timer. You can directly input "80 minutes", which will be automatically converted to 1 hour and 20 minutes. If the input is left blank, it will default to 0.

### 3. Check if Files Exist

If you have moved or deleted music files on your phone, you can first select "Update Folder" from the three dots in the upper right corner of the Folder page. The app will then re-read the music files from the media library to the Folder page. After that, you can select "Check if files exist" in the upper right corner of the Playing or Playlist page. This function compares the playlist with the Folder page data.

## Possible Issue

You may experience an issue where the music playback stops before the timer expires after you lock your device screen. This is likely due to the phone’s power management system, which may terminate apps that it deems as energy-consuming and unnecessary.

### Solution

To resolve this, go to your phone’s Settings, then find and select ‘App Management’. Locate our app from the list and check the power management settings for it. Adjust the settings to a more lenient mode to ensure that the phone does not prematurely terminate our app.

If the problem persists despite these adjustments, please feel free to provide feedback. Thank you for your support!
