## Privacy Policy

Thank you for using Lotus Player Lite! We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how our app handles your information.

### What We Do

**Lotus Player Lite is a local music player that:**
- Only reads music files from your device's media library
- Operates entirely offline (except for App Store in-app purchase functionality)
- Contains no advertisements
- Does not collect, store, or share any personal information

### Permissions

We only request permission to read your device's media library so you can access and play your music files. This is the sole permission required for the app to function.

### Network Connectivity

This app does not connect to the internet, with the only exception being the App Store's in-app purchase library, which is provided by the platform and not controlled by our app.

### Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage statistics, or any other information about you or your device.
