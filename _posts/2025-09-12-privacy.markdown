## Privacy Policy

### Our Commitment

Thank you for using Lotus Player Lite! We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing you with a pure local music experience.

### App Features

**Lotus Player Lite is a local music player:**
- **Completely Offline**: Works without internet connection, except for in-app purchases
- **Local Music Only**: Only accesses music files on your device
- **Zero Data Collection**: Does not collect, store, or share any personal information
- **Ad-Free Experience**: Enjoy pure music moments

### Permissions

This app only requests permission to access your device's media library, which is necessary for music playback.

### Network Connection

This app does not connect to the internet, with the only exception being the in-app purchase functionality provided by the app store, which is controlled by the platform.
